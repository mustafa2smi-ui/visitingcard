Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQ)

MoonStar Mall 🌙 ⭐ par shopping, payment aur delivery se judi sabhi jankari.

🔍
🛍️ Order Karne Se Jude Sawal
1. MoonStar Mall par order kaise karein? +
Aap jo bhi product YouTube par dekhte hain, website ke catalog card me jakar [Order Now] button dabayein. Product ka link, photo aur price form me automatic fill ho jayenge. Bas apna naam, 10-digit mobile number aur delivery address daal kar [Submit Order] par click kar dein.
2. Kya order karne ke liye website par account banana jaruri hai? +
Nahi, kisi bhi lambe registration ya password ki zaroorat nahi hai. Aap seedha apna delivery address aur number daal kar bina kisi login jhanjhat ke turant order book kar sakte hain.
3. Coupon discount code kaise apply karein? +
Order Form ke andar "Have a Discount Coupon?" ka box milega. Waha apna code (jaise: MOON50 ya WELCOME10) likhein aur Apply button dabayein. Discount turant aapke final bill se kam ho jayega.
💳 Payment & Cash on Delivery (COD)
4. Kya sabhi products par Cash on Delivery (COD) uplabdh hai? +
Nahi, sabhi products par COD aniwarya nahi hai. Kuch products par COD milta hai, lekin kuch electronic items, high-value gadgets ya sensitive parcel ke liye partial token advance ya 100% prepaid payment lena padta hai taaki farzi aur fake orders se bacha ja sake.
5. Kya online payment surakshit hai? +
Ji bilkul! Kisi bhi prepaid transaction ke liye secure payment methods (UPI, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm ya Bank transfer) ka istemal kiya jata hai.
🚚 Delivery & Shipping Timings
6. Mera parcel aane me kitne din lagenge? +
Samanya roop se order dispatch hone ke baad 4 se 7 business days ke andar delivery ho jati hai. Pincode daalte hi district auto-detect hota hai jisse courier delivery me koi galti nahi hoti.
7. Delivery charge kitna lagta hai? +
MoonStar Mall par sabhi standard orders par All India FREE Delivery di ja rahi hai.
⏱️ 24-Hours Return, Cancel & Rules
8. Mai apna order kaise cancel kar sakta hu? +
Order submit karne ke 24 ghante ke andar aapko website par "Return / Cancel Form" kholkar cancellation request daalni hogi. Agar parcel courier ko assign hokar dispatch ho gaya, to cancel nahi kiya ja sakega.
9. Kharab ya galat product aane par return kaise hoga? +
Delivery boy se parcel lene ke 24 ghante ke andar return request daalna jaruri hai. 24 ghante beet jaane ke baad koi bhi return claim manzoor nahi hoga.
10. Parcel unboxing video banana kyu jaruri hai? +
Unboxing Video Aniwarya (Mandatory) Hai! Parcel kholte waqt bina kisi cut ya edit ke pura video banayein jisme bahar ka shipping label aur andar ka product saaf dikhe. Courier transit me saman tutne ya gayab hone par yahi video aapke replacement ya refund ka pakka saboot hota hai.
🤝 Sell on MoonStar Mall (Vendors)
11. Kya mai apna product yaha bech sakta hu? +
Ji haan! Agar aap dukandaar, manufacturer ya YouTube creator hain, to header me diye gaye [🤝 Sell with Us] button par click karke apna shop name, WhatsApp number aur product ka link hume bhej sakte hain. Hum review karke aapka product list kar denge.
