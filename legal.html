ℹ️ About AdeenDesk

Last Updated: September 2026

AdeenDesk is a comprehensive Islamic utility dashboard designed to assist Muslims worldwide in their daily spiritual and religious practices. Our platform brings together essential Islamic tools in one accessible, user-friendly interface.

🌟 Our Mission

To provide a reliable, accurate, and easy-to-use digital platform that helps Muslims stay connected to their faith through prayer times, Islamic calendar, Quranic resources, and spiritual tools — all accessible from anywhere, at any time.

📌 What We Offer

  • 🕌 Accurate Prayer Times: Location-based prayer timings using GPS and city search, powered by the Aladhan API.
  • 📅 Dual Calendars: Gregorian and Hijri (Islamic) calendars with real-time date updates after Maghrib.
  • 🧭 Qibla Direction Finder: Compass-based Qibla direction using device sensors.
  • 📖 Quran Reader: Access to the Holy Quran with translation and recitation.
  • 📿 Digital Tasbeeh: A simple and effective dhikr counter.
  • 💰 Zakat Calculator: Helps calculate Zakat payable on savings and assets.
  • 🤲 Daily Duas: A collection of essential Islamic supplications for various occasions.
  • 📜 Hadith & Stories: Daily hadith and inspirational Islamic stories.
  • 🧠 Islamic Quiz: Interactive quizzes to test and enhance Islamic knowledge.
  • 📰 Islamic News: Curated news updates from around the Muslim world.
  • 🖼️ Image Gallery: Offline storage for Islamic charts and images.

🌍 Our Vision

We envision AdeenDesk as a trusted companion for Muslims across the globe — a digital space that combines technology with spirituality, making Islamic knowledge and practice more accessible to everyone, regardless of their location or background.

🌱 A Note from Our Team:

AdeenDesk is built with the intention of serving the Ummah. We strive to ensure accuracy and reliability in all our features. However, we are continuously improving and welcome your feedback and duas for our growth and betterment.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At AdeenDesk, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1. Location Data

With your permission, we collect your device's GPS or network-based location data to provide accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. This data is used only for the functionality you request and is not stored on our servers.

1.2. Device Information

We may collect basic device information such as browser type, operating system, and screen resolution to optimize your user experience.

1.3. User-Provided Content

Any images or notes you save in our gallery or note-taking features are stored locally on your device using IndexedDB and localStorage. We do not have access to this data.

1.4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies to store your preferences (such as language selection and Hijri date offset). These are small text files stored on your device and can be cleared at any time.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide accurate prayer times based on your location.
  • To show the correct Qibla direction.
  • To remember your language and calendar preferences.
  • To improve and personalize your experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

All user data is stored locally on your device. We do not have servers that collect or store your personal information. Any location data used for prayer times is processed in real-time and not retained.

✅ Your Data Stays With You:

AdeenDesk does not collect, sell, or share your personal information with any third parties. All your notes, images, and preferences remain securely on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to provide our features:

  • Aladhan API – for prayer time calculations.
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) – for reverse geocoding.
  • ipapi.co – for IP-based location detection (fallback).

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not share any personal data with them; only anonymized location requests are made.

5. Children's Privacy

AdeenDesk does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
