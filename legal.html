ℹ️ About Us

Welcome to Daily Islamic Dashboard. Humara maqsad har Musalman bhai aur behan tak ba-hawaala, authentic aur saheeh deeni waqtiyaat aur sahulatein pahunchana hai.

Yeh platform bilkul muft (free) hai aur daily prayer times, Hijri shamsi/qamri calendar, ahadees-e-mubaraka, nasihat aameez waqiat aur aalami Islami khabrein faraham karta hai.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Aapki privacy humare liye sabse zyada ahem hai. Hum kisi bhi qism ka zati data (Personal Data) jaise password, financial records ya private information store nahi karte.

  • Location & Prayer Times: Location permission sirf aapke shehar ke namaz ke auqaat aur qibla rukh batane ke liye device me hi istemal hoti hai.
  • Offline Storage: Tasbeeh count, uploaded chart images aur settings aapke browser ki IndexedDB / LocalStorage me mehfooz rehti hain. Humare servers par upload nahi hoti.
  • Third-Party Ads: Hum Google AdSense aur deegar meyaar-shuda advertising networks istemal kar sakte hain jo cookies ke zariye ghair-zati ads dikhate hain.
