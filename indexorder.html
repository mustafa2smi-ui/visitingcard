How it works

Enter the table or location, add each customer and their items, then tap Generate QR. The shopkeeper scans the QR code with any phone camera and sees the complete order instantly — no app to install, no account to create.

You can also tap Share to send the order link via WhatsApp or SMS, or Copy Link to paste it anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Does the shopkeeper need to install anything?

No. They scan the QR with their phone camera and the order opens in their browser.

Can multiple customers order at the same table?

Yes. Add a customer card for each person. Everything stays grouped under the same table number on the order slip.

Is my order saved?

Your order is saved in this browser only. It is not sent to any server. The QR code contains the order data itself.
