Shaam (Maghrib) ke baad Hijri Date kyu badal jaati hai?
Islamic Rule
Islami Lunar Calendar me naya din Maghrib (suraj doobne) par shuru hota hai, aadhi raat 12 baje nahi. Isliye sham ke waqt English date wahi rehti hai lekin Hijri date aur din agle din me tabdeel ho jata hai.
Kya delete hua content ya image recover ho sakti hai?
Privacy & Data
Nahi. AdeenDesk user ki privacy ka pura khayal rakhta hai. Hamare servers par aapka koi personal data ya images store nahi hoti. Browser clear hone ya delete hone par data recover nahi ho sakta.
Namaz aur Sahri/Iftar ke time me 2-3 minute ka farq kyu hota hai?
AdeenDesk GPS coordinates aur universal solar calculations use karta hai. Aapki local masjid me ehtiyaatan (precautionary) 1-2 minute pehle ya baad ka waqt rakha jata hai. Hamesha apni local azaan ko tarjeeh dein.
Kya yeh dashboard internet ke bina (Offline) kaam karta hai?
Haan! AdeenDesk ek PWA (Progressive Web App) hai. Ek baar open hone ke baad Quran, Duas, aur Hijri Calendar offline bhi smoothly kaam karte hain.

शाम (मग़रिब) के बाद हिजरी तारीख क्यों बदल जाती है?
इस्लामी नियम
इस्लामी कैलेंडर में नया दिन रात 12 बजे नहीं, बल्कि सूर्यास्त (मग़रिब) के समय शुरू होता है। इसलिए शाम होते ही अंग्रेज़ी तारीख वही रहती है लेकिन हिजरी तारीख बदल जाती है।

Why does the Hijri date change in the evening after Sunset?
Islamic Astronomical Rule
In the Islamic lunar calendar, a new day begins at Maghrib (Sunset), not at midnight 12:00 AM. Hence, the Gregorian date remains the same while the Hijri date advances.
Can deleted images or user logs be recovered?
Privacy Policy
No. AdeenDesk respects user privacy by operating as a client-side utility. No personal media or private files are stored on our servers, making recovery impossible once deleted.
Why is there a minor offset in prayer times compared to local mosques?
Times are computed using precise GPS coordinates and standard solar algorithms. Local mosques often apply a 1-3 minute precautionary window. Local congregation calls should always take precedence.
Does this dashboard work completely offline?
Yes. AdeenDesk is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Core modules like the Quran Reader, Duas, and Islamic Calendar cache locally for offline use.
