📍 Location & Roza Calculation Central India (Default)
■ Din (Daylight) ■ Raat (Night)
⚠️ Zaroori Maloomat & Wazahat (Scientific & Fiqh Note):
  • Location Factor: Yeh chart diye gaye Latitude (arz-e-balad) par mabni astronomical calculations se generate kiya jata hai.
  • Roza vs Daylight: Roza suraj nikalne se nahi balki Subah Sadiq (Fajr) se shuru hota hai aur Ghurub-e-Aftab (Maghrib) par khatam hota hai. Isliye Roze ka dauraniya suraj ke din se taqreeban 1 ghante 15 minute zyada hota hai.
  • Ahem Din: 21 June ko saal ka sabse lamba din (Summer Solstice) aur 21/22 December ko sabse chhota din (Winter Solstice) hota hai.
