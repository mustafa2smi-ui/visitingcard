Account Protection

4. Hardware Device Binding & Loss Recovery

To prevent unauthorized account resale and illegal credential sharing, SaralShareMarket operates a strict Primary Hardware Token System:

  • An active subscription is strictly bound to one mobile device or primary web browser.
  • Simultaneous login attempts or OTP verification on an unrecognized secondary device will be rejected by our backend.
  • Device Loss, Theft, or Hardware Upgrade: In genuine cases where a phone is broken or replaced, the user must contact support via their registered email. Upon manual verification, the administrator will reset the database token to allow pairing on the replacement device.

Privacy Policy

9. Privacy Policy & Minimal Data Retention

Private Portal Access & User Allocation via Email OTP:

SaralShareMarket operates as a controlled, private member platform rather than an open public web directory. Email One-Time Password (OTP) verification is strictly deployed to:

  • Private Portal Allocation: Allocate authenticated access to genuine individual learners, restricting open bot scraping, automated spam, and unauthorised anonymous browsing.
  • Token Binding & Account Isolation: Anchor the student's unique hardware device token to an active email address, preventing illegal account reselling or credential leaks.
  • Access Control: Ensure that paid features and structured curriculum roadmaps remain fully protected within a private educational environment.

We practice data minimization and do not collect unnecessary personal files. Information handled is strictly limited to:

  • Registered Email ID (for account matching and OTP communication).
  • Browser Device Token (used exclusively for single-device hardware authentication).
  • Transaction Reference numbers & UTR IDs (to record validity start and end timestamps).

We do not sell, rent, or trade student email lists to third-party marketing companies. Data is processed securely through standard Google Cloud infrastructure.
