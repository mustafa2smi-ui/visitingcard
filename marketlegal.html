Who We Are

1. About SaralShareMarket

SaralShareMarket is an educational platform designed to make stock market and financial education easier to discover, understand and follow.

The financial education landscape contains a large number of educational videos and resources published on YouTube and other platforms. Finding the right lesson, following topics in a sensible order and moving from basic concepts to advanced subjects can be difficult for learners.

SaralShareMarket aims to address this problem by organising educational resources into topic-wise categories, learning levels and structured course sequences.

What We Offer

  • Organised stock market learning topics.
  • Topic-wise educational video collections.
  • Beginner-to-advanced learning sequences.
  • Convenient access to related lessons in one place.
  • Categories covering fundamental and technical analysis.
  • Educational resources covering trading, investing, mutual funds, derivatives and other financial subjects.
  • Course discovery and navigation features.

Why Do We Charge If YouTube Videos Are Free?

The subscription fee is for our organised learning service, not a claim of ownership over free YouTube videos.

Many educational videos available through YouTube can be watched without paying the original publisher. However, finding relevant videos across hundreds or thousands of topics, identifying suitable lessons and arranging them into a useful learning sequence can require considerable time and effort.

SaralShareMarket provides a curated educational experience by organising selected learning resources into categories, arranging lessons in a structured order and making them easier to discover through a dedicated course dashboard.

Our monthly subscription fee is intended to cover the value of this organisation, curation, course structure, website functionality and convenience.

We do not charge users on the basis that we own the original YouTube videos merely because they are displayed or embedded on our website. The original creators retain their applicable rights in their content.

A subscription to SaralShareMarket does not automatically grant access to paid YouTube content, private videos, restricted third-party material or any service that requires separate payment or permission.

Our Independence

Unless expressly stated otherwise, SaralShareMarket is an independent educational platform and is not officially affiliated with YouTube, NSE, BSE, SEBI, any stockbroker, or any financial institution.
