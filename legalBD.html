About Us

Business Dost ek dedicated digital discovery aur local promotion initiative hai jiska uddeshya chhote dukandaron, local service providers, retailers aur professionals ko digital reach pradan karna hai.

Hum YouTube videos, community updates aur social media ke madhyam se local businesses ko unke aas-paas ke grahakon tak bina kisi bhari marketing kharche ke pahunchane me madad karte hain.

Terms & Conditions (Content & Promotion Guidelines)

Business Dost par apna business submit karne se pehle kripya nimnlikhit niyam dhyan se padhein:

  1. Content Ownership & Permission: Business dwara bheje gaye video, photo, poster ya logo par business owner ka poora adhikar hona anivarya hai. Kisi teesre paksh ka content bina permission use na karein.
  2. Copyrighted Audio / Songs: Video me koi bhi copyrighted commercial gana, background track ya sound effect nahi hona chahiye. YouTube copyright strike ya audio mute hone par Business Dost ki koi zimmedari nahi hogi.
  3. Tobacco & Alcohol Restrictions: Tobacco, bidi, gutkha, cigarette, alcohol, vape ya kisi bhi nishedhit nasheele padarthon ka promotion swikar nahi kiya jayega.
  4. Prohibited & Illegal Businesses: Illegal activities, betting/gambling, nakli saman, bina license wale hathiyar, fraud ya misleading schemes ko promote nahi kiya jayega.
  5. Price & Claim Accuracy: Dukan ke rates, discounts, guarantees aur offers ki satyata ki poori zimmedari business owner ki hogi.
  6. Medical & Cure Claims: "100% ilaj", "guaranteed treatment" ya bina pramanik license wale medical claims platform policies ke tahat strictly prohibited hain.
  7. No Guaranteed Views / Growth: "3 Months Free Plan" ya kisi bhi promotional plan me guaranteed views, customers, sales ya video viral hone ka koi wada ya guarantee nahi hai. Reach platform algorithms par nirbhar karti hai.
  8. YouTube Advertising Review: Kisi bhi video ka Google Ads ya internal promotion ke liye select hona YouTube/Google automated aur manual content review par nirbhar karta hai.
  9. Channel Monetization & Ad Revenue Waiver: Business Dost channel par upload hone wali video se aane wale YouTube AdSense revenue ya channel sponsorship par business ka koi adhikar ya daawa nahi hoga. Video ka AdSense revenue poorntah Business Dost channel ke paas rahega.
  10. Third-Party Content Claims: Agar business dwara use kiye gaye background music par copyright owner dwara claim kiya jata hai, toh uski revenue ya penalty ke liye business swayam zimmedar hoga.
  11. Editing Rights: Business Dost ko promotional requirements ke anusar video ko crop, trim, watermark add karne, description likhne ya contact details attach karne ka poora adhikar hoga.
  12. Right to Reject: Business Dost bina koi vishisht kaaran bataye kisi bhi video submission ko policy risk ya anuchit paye jane par reject kar sakta hai.
  13. Right to Remove / Unlist: Purane offers, galat complaint, policy violation ya legal notice milne par published video ko kisi bhi samay remove, private ya unlist kiya ja sakta hai.
  14. Promotion Period Clarification: Promotional plan (Free ya Paid) samapt hone ke baad video ko channel status ke anusar active, private ya unlisted rakhna Business Dost ke discretionary nirnay par hoga.
  15. Offer Validity & Stock: Shopkeeper dwara bataye gaye offer ki expiry date aur stock availability ki jankari customer ko dena shopkeeper ki zimmedari hai.
  16. Business Information Updates: Dukan ka pata, phone number ya opening timing badalne par business owner ko Business Dost ko suchit karna hoga.
  17. Community Tab Visibility: Community updates platform ke active distribution par nirbhar karte hain; har subscriber ko notification milne ki guarantee nahi hoti.
  18. External Social Sharing: Business Dost dwara banaye gaye post aur links ko business owner apne star par WhatsApp ya social media par share karne ke liye swatantra hai.
  19. Third-Party Distribution: Business Dost content ko YouTube ke alawa Facebook, Instagram ya local portals par cross-promote kar sakta hai.
  20. Paid Promotion Transparency: Paid service ke mamle me YouTube policy ke tahat "Paid Promotion" tag/disclosure add kiya ja sakta hai.
  21. No Endorsement: Channel par kisi dukan ka video aane ka matlab yeh nahi hai ki Business Dost unke saman ya service ki quality ka endorsement ya guarantee leta hai.
  22. Customer-Merchant Disputes: Grahak aur dukandar ke beech payment, return, refund, service dispute ya nakli saman ki shikayat ke liye Business Dost kisi bhi roop me zimmedar ya pakshkaar nahi hoga.
  23. Statutory Compliances: Dukan ka GST, trade license, FSSAI ya anya zaroori registration manage karna business ki niji zimmedari hai.
  24. Data Protection: Form me di gayi details sirf promotion aur verification ke uddeshya se istemal ki jayengi.
  25. Public Contact Display: Video ya description me diya gaya official phone/WhatsApp number public discovery ke liye upload kiya jata hai.
  26. Minor Consent: Video me kisi bachhe ya niji vyakti ki image aane par unke legal guardian ki anumati hona anivarya hai.
  27. Notice & Takedown: Kisi legal authority ya genuine brand trademark owner se complaint aane par video ko bina purv suchna ke unlist kiya ja sakta hai.
  28. Trademark Authorization: Business dwara display kiya gaya logo ya dukan ka board kisi anya register brand ka copy ya anadhikrit nahi hona chahiye.
  29. No Fake Views / Bots: Video par kisi bhi tarah ke fake views, automated traffic ya bot boosting ka istemal sakht manaa hai.
  30. Policy Modification: Business Dost kisi bhi samay platform policies ya YouTube updates ke hisab se in terms me badlaw karne ka adhikar rakhta hai.
  31. Mandatory Consent: Form submit karne wala pratyek user in sabhi niyam aur disclosure policies se poorn sehmat mana jayega.
  32. Right to Cease Service (Service Termination): Kisi bhi anaitik vyavahar, gaali-galauj ya fake identity paye jane par business ki listing ko tatkal prabhav se hamesha ke liye black-list aur remove karne ka adhikar surakshit hai.
  33. No Partner / Agent Relationship: Business Dost aur submit karne wale vyavasayi ke beech sirf promotional platform ka sambandh hai; isse koi partnership, agency, franchise ya legal employment create nahi hota.
  34. Force Majeure (Apatkalin Sthiti): Platform outage, internet ban, YouTube algorithm changes, policy block ya technical server fault ke dauran service me deri hone par platform jawabdeh nahi hoga.
  35. Jurisdiction & Dispute Resolution: Kisi bhi niyamak ya kanooni vivaad ki sthiti me nishpadan local jurisdiction ke adheen kiya jayega.
