    🎯 Swing Strategy: Actionable Checklist

    • Moving Average Alignment: Stock must trade above 20 EMA and 50 SMA for strong momentum swing trades.
    • Darvas Box Breakout: Look for a daily close above the upper Darvas Box boundary accompanied by high volume.
    • Institutional Delivery Confirmation: Delivery percentage above 45% - 50% (highlighted in Green) confirms accumulation rather than pure intraday churning.
    • Trend Strength (ADX): ADX above 25 confirms trend strength. Avoid new breakout entries if RSI is severely overbought (>75).
    • Risk & Stop-loss: Recommended Stop-Loss is trailing below the lower Darvas Box or the 20-period Moving Average. Target minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward.
